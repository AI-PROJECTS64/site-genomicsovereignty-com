GenomicSovereignty.com

Strategic descriptive .com domain for “genomic sovereignty”: the ability of States, peoples, regions or alliances to govern access to, use of and benefit-sharing around genomic resources and data — human and non-human.
Designed as a neutral banner for genomic data governance initiatives, observatories, alliances and ethical frameworks at the interface of biobanks, national genome programmes, AI in health and indigenous rights.
It offers a board-level phrase for a complex set of tensions: who controls genomic datasets, under which rules, and with which safeguards against extractive or discriminatory uses.
Serious offers only (international organisations, governments and agencies, major foundations, large research consortia and biobanks, alliances involving indigenous and community organisations).
